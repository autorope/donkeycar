Calibration warnings: these signals won't show a percentage, or may be reading against the wrong baseline.
    Do the model's "sub-networks" agree? (confidence)
    Relative to this model's own baseline, not a calibrated probability.
    This works by asking the model the same question 15 times, each time with a different random slice of its "brain" (dropout) switched off — like asking 15 slightly different clones. If the clones agree, confidence is high; if they scatter, it's low. This measures whether the model agrees with itself — not whether it's actually right.
    Does this look familiar? (novelty)
    A different signal from model confidence above.
    This checks how far the current camera view is, in the model's internal representation, from everything it saw during training — like asking "does this remind me of anything I've driven through before?" A totally new kind of scene can score high here even while the model still feels "confident" above — that's the point of having both signals.
    Is the answer stable? (TTA stability)
    A different signal from the two above.
    This shows the model the same photo 8 times, each time with the brightness/contrast/noise tweaked slightly — like showing a photo at noon, at dusk, through foggy glasses. If the answer barely changes, stability is high. A low score means the model is fragile to lighting/noise — a different failure mode from low confidence or high novelty.

    About Control Modes

    Joystick control is provided via the blue touch/click area on screen. Click or touch and drag to control throttle and steering. In joystick mode, you can also use the following keyboard keys:

    • Forward: I
    • Reverse: K
    • Left: J
    • Right: L

    Gamepad control is enabled by the HTML5 gamepad API, currently supported by Chrome and Firefox. Playstation 3 controllers have been confirmed to work.

    Device tilt control is enabled for devices with device orientation sensors, and should work with most modern smartphones. Hold your device in landscape mode, tilt forward/backward for throttle and left/right for steering.

    Pilot Mode can be toggled or chosen using the keyboard.

    • Toggle Pilot Mode: M
    • User Mode: U
    • Auto Steering Mode: S
    • Full Auto Mode: A

    Recording can be toggled using the keyboard.

    • Toggle Recording: R
