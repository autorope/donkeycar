Donkeycar Track Tents — 3″ × 3″ faces, self-standing

  1. Print at 100% / “Actual size” (turn OFF “fit to page”) on US Letter, portrait. Card stock stands better than paper.
  2. Cut along the solid outline of each 3″ × 9″ strip.
  3. Fold down on the darker dashed line in the middle, printed side OUT. That crease is the top of the tent; the two images swing down front and back, and both come out right-side-up.
  4. Fold the two 1.5″ tabs outward on their dashed lines so they lie flat on the table like feet. Tape them down — or tape the two tabs to a strip of card stock — and the tent stands on its own.
1.5" tab → folds out as a foot 3" image (printed upside down on the sheet) ==== APEX FOLD ==== 3" image 1.5" tab → folds out as a foot

Assumption: each tent shows the same image on both faces, so the car reads it identically from either approach direction. Say the word to pair two different images back-to-back.

13 tents, 2 per page → 7 sheets. (The 9″ strip only fits twice across a Letter page.) Screen-only instructions; they won’t print.
