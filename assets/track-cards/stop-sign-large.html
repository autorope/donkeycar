Large stop sign, for measuring distance

The same stop sign as the track cards, with the octagon 4.5 inches across its flats instead of 2 1/16, so the car's camera can measure it from further away.
  1. Print at 100% / "Actual size" on one sheet of US Letter, with background graphics on. Turn off "fit to page".
  2. Check with a ruler that the octagon measures 4.5 inches across its flat sides, dark red border included. Write down what you measure: that is the size to give measure_object_distance.
  3. Fold in half on the dashed line, printed side out. Both faces read upright.
  4. Stand it in a card stand with the fold at the top. The stand grips the 0.6 inches below each sign, so the octagon stays fully in view. Check from the side that it stands vertical.
